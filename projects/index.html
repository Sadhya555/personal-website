ESP32 Pulse Oximeter & Heart Rate Monitor

ESP32 · MAX30102 · SSD1306 OLED

A pulse oximeter and heart-rate monitor built around an ESP32, a MAX30102 optical sensor, and a 0.96" OLED display. Separates the AC and DC components of the raw optical signal in firmware to estimate SpO2 and heart rate in real time.
