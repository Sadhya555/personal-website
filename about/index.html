A bit more about me

I'm a 3rd-year Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at BRAC University in Dhaka, expected to graduate in early 2029. My work outside coursework centers on embedded systems and robotics.

I'm a part of BRACU Mongol-Tori, the university's competitive Mars rover team and student research project team, where I work in Controls & Software, built to compete at the University Rover Challenge (URC) and European Rover Challenge (ERC).

Beyond the rover, I'm particularly interested in biomedical engineering, bionics, prosthetics, and neuroengineering. Currently my only project, the pulse oximeter, is one related to my interests; the project sits somewhere along that line between embedded systems and sensing the human body.
